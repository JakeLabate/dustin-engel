The E5 Framework

Five drivers of enterprise value.

E5 is the enterprise value system I built at Elegant Disruption. It strengthens the five drivers of performance that investors and buyers actually price: growth, innovation, efficiency, resilience, and reputation.

The framework did not start from theory. It came from analyzing more than 100 due diligence checklists to find what acquirers and investors consistently prioritize when they value a business.

The five drivers

What investors and buyers actually price.

01

Growth

Revenue trajectory, retention, and market share. A business that keeps compounding is worth more than one that keeps starting over.

02

Innovation

Adaptability and new value creation. The capacity to lead change in your market rather than react to it.

03

Efficiency

Operational discipline and margins. Streamlined processes that produce more output with fewer resources.

04

Resilience

Governance and risk management. An operation built to hold up in any climate, not just the current one.

05

Reputation

Brand equity and trust. Positioning and proof that hold up with customers, markets, and the people writing the check.

How it works

From audit to execution.

Audit

A comprehensive examination of the business across all five drivers: where value is compounding and where it is leaking.

Benchmark

Performance measured against what investors and buyers in your market actually expect, so priorities are set by evidence rather than instinct.

Align

Tailored recommendations and leadership alignment sessions, so the team agrees on where resources go before execution starts.

Execute

Hands-on consulting support to implement the recommendations, with the follow-through to make progress visible.

Why this lens

Value over motion.

Plenty of work creates activity. E5 exists to tie day-to-day decisions to what actually compounds: the enterprise value of the business. That is the same discipline behind every engagement, whether or not E5 is run formally.
