Writing

When the systems underneath a business move.

Search is fragmenting. Third-party data is going away. Marketing stacks have outgrown their own usefulness. Pricing has quietly become a trust signal.

I have been writing about that shift since 2017, in CMSWire, MarTechCube, MyTotalRetail, and MediaPost. It is the same problem underneath most of the decisions I get called into, which is why the advisory work and the writing tend to say the same thing.

Transparency is the New Loyalty: Why Brands Should Treat Tariffs Like Sales Tax
