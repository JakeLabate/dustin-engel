Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Who do you work with?

Founders and leadership teams, typically at companies in the $7.5M to $25M range, who are navigating real inflection points: an exit on the horizon, a strategic decision that will not wait, or a market being reshaped by AI.

What does working with you actually look like?

There are two main ways we work together. An assessment is a structured engagement where we look honestly at where value is compounding, where it is leaking, and which decisions matter most over the next 90 days. Advisory is ongoing support for teams that want a steady outside perspective and tighter follow-through.

What do you mean by responding to AI without hype?

Most of the AI conversation right now is noisy, either breathless excitement or vague fear. Neither is useful when you are trying to run a business. The useful work is grounding AI choices in your actual decisions and economics, and tightening execution where it accelerates enterprise value.

Why enterprise value?

Enterprise value is shorthand for what actually compounds over time, versus what just creates motion.

How is this different from a typical management consultant?

No team of analysts, and no 90-page report. The work is hands-on, direct, and focused on the decisions in front of you right now.
